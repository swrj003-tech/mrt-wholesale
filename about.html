Corporate Overview

Sourcing built on trust, powered by direct lanes.

At MRT International Holding LLC, we operate as a specialized global trading and procurement agency, bridging high-volume retail buyers, contract distributors, and scaling private-label brands with verified manufacturers. Registered in Wyoming, USA, with our primary logistics, sourcing, and field inspection desk centered in Dubai, UAE, we absorb the regulatory, operational, and administrative complexities of cross-border trade.

Our Dubai operations office maintains direct relationships on factory floors throughout major manufacturing hubs in Southeast Asia, India, and the Middle East. By maintaining a physical presence where production occurs, we eliminate broker markups, enforce strict raw-material and laboratory certification checks, and manage container dispatch under standardized ICC Incoterms.

Compliance & Control

The Quality Assurance Framework

Our dedicated field agents execute rigorous supplier inspections and logistical oversight across four essential phases of production.

domain

1. Supplier Pre-Qualification

On-site audit of machinery logs, worker safety protocols, chemical control compliance, and financial stability verification before drafting trade agreements.

view_in_ar

2. In-Line Material Inspections

Rigorous checks during production (at 30% and 80% marks). Testing raw material tensile strength, dimensions, and assembly tolerances against target designs.

assignment_turned_in

3. Lab Compliance Testing

Coordinating and auditing laboratory testing with certified third-party testing agencies (SGS, Bureau Veritas) to verify FCC, CE, RoHS, FDA, or GOTS requirements.

inventory

4. Loading & Container Sealing

On-site supervision during packing to prevent humidity damage, verify secure carton stacking, and apply high-security ISO 17712 bolt seals prior to customs entry.
