Sourcing Guidelines

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

expand_more
Due to our direct-to-factory contract structures, our standard wholesale MOQ is typically **5,000 units** per line item. For high-value electronics, certified custom formulas, or proprietary ODM packaging molds, alternative MOQs can be custom coordinated through our account desks.

Can we request pre-production physical samples?

expand_more
Yes, absolutely. Once initial pricing quotes are aligned and raw material availability is verified, MRT coordinates physical manufacturing sample dispatch to your headquarters for quality verification, sizing control, and custom testing before mass production contracts are signed.

How is manufacturing quality controlled?

expand_more
Every production batch undergoes a mandatory 3-point inspection: (1) Raw material verification, (2) Inline manufacturing audit, and (3) A final pre-shipment inspection (PSI) performed by our third-party certified quality control partners to ensure adherence to your specific product brief.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

expand_more
We standardly conduct B2B operations under **FOB**, **CIF**, or **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** terms. Commercial payment cycles follow a standard 30% manufacturing start deposit, with the remaining 70% balance cleared upon successful pre-shipping quality audit verification.
