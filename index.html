Trading & Procurement Protocols

The Sourcing Framework

Bypassing standard B2B middle-men and brokers. We establish direct factory contracts, manage on-site pre-shipment inspections, and coordinate complete customs clearance from end to end.

factory

On-Site Production Audits

We verify factory capability directly on the production floor. Our local agents conduct machinery calibration reviews, raw material supply chain audits, and social compliance checks before drafting contract terms.

gavel

Compliance & Lab Testing

We manage materials certification from the source. We coordinate third-party laboratory trials for product lines, ensuring all exports meet EPA, FCC, CE, RoHS, or FDA requirements before cargo dispatch.

receipt_long

Trade Finance & Escrow

Secure contract administration. All agreements operate under standardized ICC Incoterms 2020 (FOB, CIF, DDP), utilizing bank-hosted commercial escrows to protect capital during manufacturing phases.

anchor

Consolidation & Logistics

Streamlining the maritime corridor. We handle Less-than-Container Load (LCL) and Full Container Load (FCL) consolidation, customs entry filings, export licenses, and final-mile domestic dispatch.
